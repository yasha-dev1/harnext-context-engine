# Arm `F3` — reorganized store, preserve version

Source: Zhou et al. §3: *"Reorganized store has an LLM restructure the Verbatim dump, splitting and
merging content across sessions into files whose grouping the model chooses … adding one 'keep every
fact' rule gives the Reorg. (preserve) version."* App. A.2: reorganizer prompt, *"a dedicated
restructuring stage of up to five passes over the store"*. Plan row F3: *"F0 rewritten weekly into
per-entity and topic files under a 'keep every fact' rule; the consolidation-cadence design."*
Implementation: `stores/llm_files.py:312-352`, prompt `prompts.py:69-73`, `99-101`, `144-147`.
Shared decisions: [README.md](README.md).

## What an examiner should know

- F3 is the **dump-then-consolidate** lane (plan row 144): F0 quality between passes, curated
  quality after. At a checkpoint boundary a pass always runs (`last_of_month`), so every
  `ckpt-YYYY-MM` is post-reorganization unless that pass failed twice (then `_intake/` is non-empty
  and `build-log.jsonl` says so).
- Each pass rewrites *existing* organized files too (rule 3), so F3's store history is the most
  volatile of the arms; costs scale with store size, not only with intake size.
- The `bytes_before/bytes_after` series is the paper's compression diagnostic; report it.
- Because passes cover ~100 events, the "Reorganized store" here is closer to a coarse-window
  curated store than to the paper's single restructuring of a whole conversation. Say so.
- Prompt hash covers all of this (`prompts.digest()`); `reorg_every` is in `build-state.json:config`.
